Over 29{,}000 verified records across 195 countries and regions have been migrated to the AUDA analytical database. There are 39 different groups of features, including demographic features such as population and GDP, waste management features such as plastic waste generation and mismanaged plastic waste, and plastic consumption features such as total resin consumption and polymer PET consumption.

Although all records are verified by humans, the data points are still subject to measurement errors, reporting inconsistencies across countries and regions, and the use of different statistical methods by different organizations. Additionally, many data groups do not have enough data points to be used for modeling. Therefore, we select a subset of datasets that are suitable for modeling and analysis in this study.

This study primarily utilizes datasets in the AUDA analytical database, as summarized in Table~\ref{tab:datasets}. The \texttt{YearPWG} dataset captures the annual trend in plastic waste generation; the \texttt{YearTRC} dataset represents the annual trend in total resin consumption; and the \texttt{YearPPC} dataset reflects the annual trend in polymer PET consumption. The \texttt{PWDriverFeatureSet} dataset is an unlabeled dataset comprising population, GDP, urban population, plastic waste generation, mismanaged plastic waste, and the proportion of plastic waste that is properly managed. The \texttt{PWGPredictors} dataset includes the features: year, GDP, population, urban population, rural population, and the rural population ratio. The \texttt{PWDrivers} dataset contains year, GDP, urban population, and rural population. The \texttt{PWDrivers (Japan)} and \texttt{PWDrivers (Slovenia)} datasets are subsets of the \texttt{PWDrivers} dataset, containing data specific to Japan and Slovenia, respectively.

An additional dataset is obtained from \textit{Our World in Data}, which provides the annual trend in global plastics production~\cite{owid_global_plastics_production}.
