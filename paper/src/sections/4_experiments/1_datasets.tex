This study primarily utilizes datasets migrated from the PRISM database, as summarized in Table~\ref{tab:datasets}. The \texttt{YearPWG} dataset captures the annual trend in plastic waste generation; the \texttt{YearTRC} dataset represents the annual trend in total resin consumption; and the \texttt{YearPPC} dataset reflects the annual trend in polymer PET consumption. The \texttt{PWDriverFeatureSet} dataset is an unlabeled dataset comprising population, GDP, urban population, plastic waste generation, mismanaged plastic waste, and the proportion of plastic waste that is properly managed. The \texttt{PWGPredictors} dataset includes the features: year, GDP, population, urban population, rural population, and the rural population ratio. The \texttt{PWDrivers} dataset contains year, GDP, population, urban population, rural population, urban population ratio, and rural population ratio. The \texttt{PWDrivers (Japan)} and \texttt{PWDrivers (Slovenia)} datasets are subsets of the \texttt{PWDrivers} dataset, containing data specific to Japan and Slovenia, respectively.

An additional dataset is obtained from \textit{Our World in Data}, which provides the annual trend in global plastics production~\cite{owid_global_plastics_production}.
