\subsection{Data Migration to AUDA}

AUDA utilizes a script to migrate up-to-date approved data points from PRISM into its analytical database through an Extract-Transform-Load (ETL) process. This migration stage reorganizes validated PRISM records into an analytical schema where all tables are keyed by year and location. During the migration, AUDA applies multiple quality-preserving constraints to ensure data integrity and analytical reliability. This includes excluding null-valued records, enforcing referential integrity on geographic attributes, and maintaining consistency in units and data types.

All analyses and experiments in this study will exclusively use data from the AUDA analytical database.
