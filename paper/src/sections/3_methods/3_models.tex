\subsection{Models}

Experiments in this paper are mainly forecasting, reconstruction, correlation analysis, and importance analysis. For the first two types of experiment, we introduce some commonly used baseline models and machine learning models.

For the forecasting tasks, AUDA preserves chronological order. Each experiment uses the first 90\% of observations for training and reserves the final 10\% of the series, with a minimum of two observations, for hold-out evaluation. The forecasting suite compares naive persistence, drift baseline, Holt-style exponential smoothing, polynomial ridge regression \cite{hoerl1970ridge}, Gaussian process regression with an RBF plus white-noise kernel, RBF-kernel support vector regression, local Theil-Sen regression over the most recent seven years.
