\subsection{Use of Generative AI}

The authors used ChatGPT to help polish the manuscript and improve its clarity. They also used Codex (various models, primarily GPT-6-Luna for the final revision) to revise and review the analysis code. The authors developed the codebase’s overall architecture and core components without AI assistance.
The authors reviewed and revised the suggested text and code, checked the reported results against the experimental outputs, and made the final methodological and interpretive decisions.
The use of large language models for data extraction within PRISM is described separately in the data-extraction subsection.
