\subsection{Experiment 4: Hyperparameter-selection Sensitivity and Robustness}

In experiment 4, we evaluate the sensitivity and robustness of the hyperparameter selection procedure to the standard-error tolerance coefficient \(c_{\mathrm{SE}}\) and the complexity ordering. We perform a sweep over \(c_{\mathrm{SE}} \in \{0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1\}\) and compare the selected models to the best-performing models in terms of WAPE on the test set. We also evaluate the robustness of the complexity ordering by comparing the selected models to the best-performing models in terms of WAPE on the test set.
