\subsection{Datasets}

Over 28{,}000 verified records across 195 countries and regions have been migrated to the AUDA analytical database. There are 39 different groups of features, including demographic features such as population and GDP, waste management features such as plastic waste generation and mismanaged plastic waste, and plastic consumption features such as total resin consumption and polymer polyethylene terephthalate (PET) consumption.

Although all records are verified by humans, the data points are still subject to measurement errors, reporting inconsistencies across countries and regions, and the use of different statistical methods by different organizations. Additionally, many data groups do not have enough data points to be used for modeling. Therefore, we select a subset of datasets that are suitable for modeling and analysis in this study.

This study primarily utilizes datasets in the AUDA analytical database, as summarized in Table~\ref{tab:datasets}. The \texttt{PWDriverFeatureSet} dataset is an unlabeled dataset comprising population, GDP, urban population, plastic waste generation, mismanaged plastic waste, and the proportion of plastic waste that is properly managed. The \texttt{PWGPredictors} dataset includes year, GDP, population, urban population, rural population, urban population ratio, and the rural population ratio as predictors for plastic waste generation. The \texttt{YearTRC (Japan)} and \texttt{YearTRC (US)} datasets represent annual trends in total resin consumption for Japan and the United States, respectively. The \texttt{GlobalPlasticsProduction} dataset is obtained from \textit{Our World in Data} and provides the annual trend in global plastics production~\cite{owid_global_plastics_production}. The \texttt{YearPWG (Japan)} and \texttt{YearPWG (US)} datasets capture annual trends in plastic waste generation for Japan and the United States, respectively. The \texttt{PWDrivers} dataset contains year, GDP, urban population, and rural population as predictors for plastic waste generation. The \texttt{PWDrivers (Japan)} and \texttt{PWDrivers (Slovenia)} datasets are subsets of the \texttt{PWDrivers} dataset, containing data specific to Japan and Slovenia, respectively.
