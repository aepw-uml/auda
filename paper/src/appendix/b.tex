\section[\appendixname~\thesection]{Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:appendix_b}

Let \(y_i\) be the ground truth value, \(\hat{y}_i\) be the predicted value, \(\bar{y}\) be the mean of the observed values, and \(n\) be the number of test samples.

Mean absolute error (MAE) measures the average absolute residual:
\begin{equation}
	\text{MAE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left| y_i - \hat{y}_i \right|.
\end{equation}

Root mean squared error (RMSE) measures the square root of the average squared residual:
\begin{equation}
	\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{
		\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n} \left( y_i - \hat{y}_i \right)^2
	}.
\end{equation}

The coefficient of determination \(R^2\) compares the residual sum of squares with the total variation in the observed values:
\begin{equation}
	R^2 = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( y_i - \hat{y}_i \right)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( y_i - \bar{y} \right)^2}.
\end{equation}

Weighted absolute percentage error (WAPE) divides the total absolute residual by the sum of the absolute observed values:
\begin{equation}
	\text{WAPE} = 100\% \times
	\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \left| y_i - \hat{y}_i \right|}
	{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \left| y_i \right|}.
\end{equation}

Symmetric mean absolute percentage error (sMAPE) averages the absolute residual scaled by the sum of the absolute observed and predicted values:
\begin{equation}
	\text{sMAPE} = \frac{100\%}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n}
	\frac{2\left| y_i - \hat{y}_i \right|}
	{|y_i| + |\hat{y}_i|}.
\end{equation}
